AUDIT: Confidence Score Analysis

1. HOW CONFIDENCE IS SET:
   - Signal dataclass has: confidence: float = 0.5 (default)
   - Set by strategy's generate() method
   - Used in selector.score() as one of 5 weighted factors
   - Weight: 0.15 out of total ~1.0

2. HOW CONFIDENCE IS USED:
   - In selector.score(): total = weighted_sum + w_confidence * conf
   - min_signal_confidence = 0.55 filters weak signals
   - Signals sorted by confidence for backtesting
   - Ranking factor, NOT a probability

3. WHAT IT REPRESENTS:
   - NOT a probability of target hit
   - NOT a heuristic in the traditional sense
   - A COMPOSITE WEIGHT for ranking signals
   - Combined with expectancy (0.35), PF (0.20), WR (0.15), regime (0.15)

4. THE SCORE METHOD WEIGHTS:
   expectancy: 0.35 (mapped from raw expectancy_r)
   profit_factor: 0.20 (normed by /2.0)
   win_rate: 0.15 (raw rate)
   regime_fit: 0.15 (regime compatibility)
   confidence: 0.15 (the signal's confidence field)

CONCLUSION: Confidence is a SIGNAL RANKING WEIGHT, NOT a probability.
